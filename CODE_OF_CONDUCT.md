# Code of conduct

We want this to be a welcoming project. Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this, and may block people who repeat it. Report concerns by email to a maintainer listed in `CITATION.cff`.

This statement is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
